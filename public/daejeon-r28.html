Kim Bong-soo started on the bench, and out of possession it was 4-4-2

After the break Ludwigson came in off the touchline into the half-space. Daejeon used that space twice — in the 47th minute Lee Myung-jae ran into the vacated flank; in the 65th Ludwigson ran into the half-space with Kang Ji-hoon behind him.

Part 3 · Diagnosis · Problem A27-match season · squad structure

The left is the main route — and only one Seo Jin-su type can finish it

Not a verdict on one loss in the 10th minute. Four reasons overlap.

Reason
01The left is the main attacking route. 11/14 successful left-side entries after the break; both open-play goals start with Anton → Lee Myung-jae. The quality of the player at the end of that route decides the outcome.
02Ludwigson — one wide role, falling output. Zero starts up front in the 4-4-2 phase. Shots/90 1.84 → 1.41 → 1.23 (down two years running) · goals + assists/90 0.60 → 0.26 → 0.37. The 10th-minute loss is one frame of a 0.34 ball-retention season.
03The cost of pinning Seo Jin-su to one spot. He plays left, centre or up front. Fix him on the left and Daejeon lose the card that fills whichever gap opens.
04Seo Jin-su alone cannot last a season. 1,446 minutes in 2026 (25 matches). Rotation needs a second player of the same type.
